# References and assets

Reviewed October 26, 2026. Reference material informed presentation; no competitor copy, reviews, trademarks or site source was incorporated.

## Restaurant patterns

- [Biscuit Love](https://www.biscuitlove.com/): visually inspected food-led hero, prominent menu/order navigation, signature-dish imagery, catering access and a short brand story. Clover Bend uses its own fictional identity and printed-menu/ticket concept.
- [Snooze](https://www.snoozeeatery.com/): accessible page content informed menu/category and seasonal ordering patterns. Its rendered browser page was blocked by Cloudflare, so no full visual-review claim is made.
- [One Page Love menu examples](https://onepagelove.com/section/menu): reference discovery and menu layout patterns, not a source for business facts.

The design direction drawn with Taste was “The page is the object,” seed 2087559787: printed menu, framed chalkboard, order pad, personal letter and gift stationery. User-requested cream, deep brown and tomato red take precedence over the skill's default palette preferences. Imbue supplies the condensed café display type; Albert Sans carries functional menu and form text. The footer is a large closing wordmark, not a separate application screen.
